
# QC-AFQMC: Quantum-Classical Auxiliary-Field Quantum Monte Carlo

A hybrid quantum-classical algorithm that leverages quantum computers to reduce 
bias in fermionic Quantum Monte Carlo simulations.

## Overview

QC-AFQMC combines the strengths of two approaches:
- **Classical AFQMC**: an accurate and scalable quantum Monte Carlo method
- **Quantum computation**: used to evaluate wavefunction overlaps with 
  richer trial wavefunctions than classical methods can efficiently handle
